# Model Card

## Intended Use
Real-time fraud risk scoring for peer-to-peer payment events.

## Inputs
Payment attributes, recipient history, customer behavior, authentication telemetry, device intelligence, behavioral biometrics, and graph risk features.

## Outputs
P2P fraud probability, recommended action, risk tier, and reason codes.

## Limitations
The model supports fraud decisioning and investigation prioritization. Human review and governance controls should be used for high-impact decisions, blocked payments, and escalations.
